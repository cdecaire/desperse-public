# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Desperse is for crypto-native creators and collectors who already understand Web3 and want a fast, polished place to publish, discover, mint, collect, and manage creative work on Solana. They use the product in focused browsing and creation sessions where media quality, speed, and ownership signals matter more than explanatory chrome.

## Product Purpose

Desperse is a content-first Web3 social media platform on Solana. It gives creators a sharp publishing and commerce surface for posts, collectibles, and editions, while giving collectors a familiar gallery and social browsing experience that quietly handles wallet, minting, and transaction complexity.

## Brand Personality

Minimal. Focused. Sharp. The interface should feel precise, restrained, and art-forward: confident through clarity rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful or bubbly UI, excessive animations, neon glow effects, busy dashboards, wallet jargon in primary flows, hex-address-first presentation, and "Web3 for Web3's sake" aesthetics. Do not let interface chrome compete with media.

## Design Principles

- Content over chrome: creator work is the primary surface; navigation and metadata support it.
- Speed is a feature: interactions should feel instant, with skeletons and optimistic feedback where appropriate.
- Progressive disclosure: abstract blockchain complexity until the user needs detail.
- Earned density: feeds and galleries breathe; settings and admin surfaces can be compact.
- Systematic consistency: use the established Desperse tokens, layout rhythm, and component vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard navigation for interactive controls, and reduced-motion alternatives for non-essential animation. Mobile touch targets should respect the platform floor: 44px on iOS-style surfaces and 48px on Android-style surfaces where applicable.
